# Venture Experiment

## Causal claim

We believe **college students renting off-campus housing** experience **difficulty protecting their security deposits from charges for pre-existing damage** because **they often lack an organized, timestamped record of the property's condition when they move in**. If offered **a $19.99 guided move-in inspection that organizes photos and creates a standardized property condition report**, we expect **renters to show purchase intent by selecting the $19.99 service and starting the inspection flow** because **it gives them organized evidence they can use to dispute unfair damage charges and protect hundreds of dollars in security-deposit money**.

## Precommitted decision rule

- Target segment: College students who currently rent, or expect to rent within the next 12 months, an off-campus house or apartment with a security deposit.
- Test action: Click the “Protect My Deposit — $19.99” call-to-action after viewing the RentGuard offer.
- Planned denominator: At least 30 eligible target-segment visitors who view the landing page.
- Threshold written before data: We will consider the test promising if at least 15% of eligible visitors click the $19.99 call-to-action.
- If the result is below the threshold, we will: Revise the value proposition, pricing, or explanation of the service and run another test before investing more time in the full product.
- If the result meets the threshold, we will: Continue developing the MVP and test a stronger form of commitment, such as beginning a guided inspection flow or joining a launch waitlist.

## Ethics and consent

- What the participant is told before acting: RentGuard is an early-stage student venture being tested. Clicking the call-to-action will not charge the participant or complete a purchase.
- Data collected: Aggregate counts of eligible visitors and call-to-action clicks, plus any voluntary non-sensitive feedback collected manually for the class experiment.
- Data deliberately not collected: Names, email addresses, payment information, precise location, lease documents, property addresses, residence photos, or other personally identifying or sensitive information.
- Retention/deletion plan: Only anonymous aggregate test results will be retained for the class evidence package. Any temporary notes containing identifying information will be deleted after the experiment is summarized.
- Why this test creates no unreasonable deception, pressure, or privacy risk: The page clearly identifies RentGuard as an early-stage concept, does not charge participants, does not require sensitive information, and allows participants to leave at any time without consequence.
