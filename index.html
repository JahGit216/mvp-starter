The problem

Your move-out dispute can start on move-in day.

Most renters take a few scattered photos, forget what they documented, or cannot quickly connect a charge months later to proof from the day they moved in. RentGuard turns that messy workaround into a repeatable process.

01

Damage is already there

Scuffs, stains, broken fixtures, and wear can exist before you ever unpack.

02

Proof gets scattered

Camera rolls and group chats are not organized property records.

03

Months later, memory loses

When deposit charges arrive, clear documentation matters more than “I remember that being there.”

How RentGuard works

A better record in three simple steps.

1

Walk room by room

Follow a guided checklist so you remember walls, floors, appliances, fixtures, windows, and other easy-to-miss areas.

2

Document what you see

Capture photos and notes in context instead of leaving evidence buried in your camera roll.

3

Create your condition report

Turn the walkthrough into a standardized record you can retain with your lease documents and reference later.

What you get

Evidence that is easier to understand when it matters.

RentGuard is designed to make the condition of your rental clear at the beginning of the lease—not reconstruct it after a disagreement starts.
