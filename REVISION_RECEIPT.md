## In-class completion checklist

After the first practice/test round, replace the pending fields with:

1. the strongest actual behavior observed;
2. the first recurring confusion or friction;
3. the exact sentence, screen, CTA, price, or workflow element changed;
4. what happened with a fresh participant after the revision; and
5. whether the revision increased, reduced, or did not change the relevant behavior.

Do not describe a change as evidence unless a participant actually encountered the revised version.
